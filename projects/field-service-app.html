The problem

A home-repair business runs on calls and texts: quotes in one place, the schedule in someone's head, hours on paper. That makes it hard to see which jobs actually made money.

How a job moves

  1. 1

    Quote

    Every job starts as a quote with no date. The owner sends it as a link and the client signs on a phone.

  2. 2

    Schedule

    The date is agreed once the client accepts, with an arrival window. Only then is the crew assigned, and the client gets a message saying who is coming and when.

  3. 3

    Work

    The crew only taps in and out. Location is taken at that tap, and the owner confirms they are in the right area. Only the owner decides when a job is finished.

  4. 4

    Close and pay

    Hours feed profit and loss. Each week the owner closes the period and every worker approves their hours. Invoices go out as a link and are marked paid by card, Zelle or check.

What it covers

  • Quotes and invoices as PDFs, with each company's own logo and colors.
  • A separate space and users for every company.
  • Calendar and repeating jobs.
  • Photos attached to quotes.
  • Customer addresses confirmed on a map.
  • Card payments through Stripe, in test mode for now.
  • English and Spanish, installable on a phone.
