Website and photo admin

Lee Home Services

A fast, mobile-first site for a home-repair business in the Bay Area. It turns Yelp referrals and Google searches into quote requests, and the owner adds project photos from his phone through a private admin panel.

Client
Lee Home Services, home repair in the Bay Area
My role
Design, development and hosting setup
Stack
HTML, CSS and JavaScript on Cloudflare Workers, with D1 and R2
Live site
leehomeservices.com

The goal

Lee gets most of his work from referrals and Yelp. The site had to look trustworthy on a phone within seconds and make asking for a quote a single tap.

What I built

  • Service pages for plumbing, electrical and remodeling, each with a photo gallery.
  • A before-and-after case study of a full-home remodel.
  • A quote form that lands in the owner's inbox, with a backup route if the first one fails.
  • Yelp reviews on the page, because that's where his customers already trust him.
  • Call and text buttons that stay within reach on every screen size.
  • Local search markup (Schema.org) and sharing tags.
  • A private admin panel where Lee adds and organizes project photos from any device, protected by an emailed login code.
  • Rebuilt around the client's new logo and orange-and-charcoal palette.

Under the hood

The site runs on Cloudflare Workers, with photo data in D1 and images in R2. The code lives on GitHub, and every change to the main branch publishes by itself.
